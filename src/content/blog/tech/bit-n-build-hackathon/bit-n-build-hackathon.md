## 🏆 WeCode Wins Bit N Build

Thrilled to announce our big win! Our team **WeCode** — Prajwal D P, Kavyashree Devraj, Samarth Sutrave, and myself — participated in **Bit N Build**, a 24-hour hackathon organized by **GDG UVCE × GDG CRCE**.

Competing in the **Web Development Track**, we built an innovative **Digital Closet** webpage with a unique approach to optimizing your digital wardrobe. We emerged as **winners** at the **state level**, securing our spot among the **top 30 teams** advancing to the **International finals in Mumbai**!

## The Project: Digital Closet

Our Digital Closet helps users organize their wardrobe by tracking item usage, offering outfit suggestions, and enabling item donation through community exchanges. It was an incredible 24 hours of collaboration, creativity, and problem-solving.
